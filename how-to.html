How to Plan a DIY Patio

Before buying pavers or digging into the yard, decide how you want to use the space. Consider furniture, walking paths, drainage, and how the patio will connect to your home and garden.

Basic steps

  1. Measure the area.
  2. Mark the patio shape.
  3. Choose your paving material.
  4. Prepare and level the base.
  5. Install the pavers.

How to Create a Garden Path

A garden path does more than move people through the yard. It can create structure and lead the eye toward important areas of the landscape. You should think about how to create a little mystery and intrigue by using curves, changes in elevation, and unexpected plantings.

Before you begin

Walk through your yard and notice the routes people already naturally take. Those routes are often the best locations for permanent pathways.

How to Build a Layered Garden Bed

Professional-looking garden beds often use plants of different heights. Place taller shrubs toward the back, medium plants in the middle, and shorter plants along the front edge.

Repeating several plants throughout the bed creates a more organized design than planting one of everything. Repeating colors along the bed gives the eye a place to rest.
